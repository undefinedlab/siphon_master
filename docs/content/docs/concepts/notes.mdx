A **note** is the unit of private balance inside a Siphon vault — not a visible wallet balance on-chain.

## At a glance

Each note represents a private claim on vault liquidity. Spending credentials are generated at deposit time and remain under your control. On-chain observers see pool activity, not which deposit you are spending.

## Deposits

When you deposit through the app:

1. Assets move into the vault for the selected token.
2. A private commitment is recorded in the pool state.
3. Spend credentials are created and kept on your side of the trust boundary.

## Spends

Spending requires a zero-knowledge proof that you own a valid note, without revealing which one. The protocol marks a note as spent so it cannot be reused.

## Spendable balance

Your balance is reconstructed from private notes matched against indexed pool state, minus anything already spent. The app handles this reconciliation — you do not manage Merkle paths manually.

## Vault operations

| Operation | What it does |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Withdraw** | Exit to a recipient address |
| **Merge** | Consolidate multiple notes |
| **Swap** | Convert between supported assets privately |
| **Split** | Divide one note into smaller pieces |
