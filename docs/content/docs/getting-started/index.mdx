Welcome to Siphon. This guide covers the basics before you deposit or build a strategy.

## Prerequisites

- A Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Rainbow, etc.)
- ETH or USDC on a supported network
- The [Siphon dapp](https://siphon.money) open in your browser

## Quick flow

1. **Connect** your wallet and select a network.
2. **Deposit** ETH or USDC into the privacy vault. A private note is created on your side — only you can spend it.
3. **Build** a strategy on the canvas or via the AI assistant (limit buy, DCA loop, stop-loss, etc.).
4. **Encrypt** strategy bounds locally. Plaintext trigger levels never leave your device.
5. **Run** — register the strategy and arm it for monitoring.
6. **Monitor & trigger** — when conditions are met, authorize execution. The runtime handles private settlement and any swap you defined.

## Your credentials stay local

Strategy encryption and spend authorization are designed around client-side control. Sensitive material is not uploaded as plaintext. You remain in the loop for trigger confirmation unless an autonomous path is enabled.
